Traditional Knife Sharpening

Knife sharpening using traditional methods uses a hard or rough surface such as a whet stone or diamond plated stone to precisely create a sharp edge on a knife, then a leather strop can be used to polish and refine the edge. These methods allow for more control over the angle and pressure, resulting in a sharper knife. Some modern methods like machines or pull-through gadgets are not good for your knife and simply don't do a good job. They can overheat a blade, grind unevenly, and damage the edge. This approach can cause your knife to dull quickly and shorten the lifespan of your knife. We use traditional knife sharpening to skillfully make knives sharper than they have ever been. And they will keep the edge sharper longer too.
