Traditional Knife Sharpening

Knife sharpening using traditional methods uses a hard or rough surface such as a whet stone or diamond plated stone to precisely create a sharp edge on a knife, then a leather strop can be used to polish and refine the edge. These methods allow for more control over the angle and pressure, resulting in a sharper knife.

Don't be fooled by quick modern methods like sharpening wheels, griders, or pull-through gadgets. Quick doesn't mean good. They are can really do alot of damage to your blade. Most knife blades are heat treated for hardness. If a blade overheats on a wheel it can complete undo that treatment leaving you with a softer metal. And it's real easy to grind a blade unevenly and ruin an edge. Don't let anybody do your knife this way.

We use traditional knife sharpening with a stone and leather to carefully and skillfully bring the blade to its full potential. Your knife will be sharper than it's ever been, guaranteed. And it'll keep an edge longer too!
